# Data-Driven Science and Engineering

Steven Brunton and Nathan Kutz bring together machine learning, optimization, and dynamical systems to give a unified view of data-driven modeling for engineering.

## What it covers

- Dimensionality reduction and matrix decompositions (SVD, POD, DMD)
- Sparse sensing and compressed sensing
- Neural networks and physics-informed learning
- Applications across fluid mechanics, control, and scientific computing

## Takeaway

A bridge book between classical scientific computing and modern ML — exactly the mindset needed for physics-informed models.
