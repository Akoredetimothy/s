# Deep Learning

Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville's *Deep Learning* (the "Deep Learning Book") is the canonical reference for the mathematical and practical foundations of the field.

## What it covers

- Linear algebra, probability, and numerical computation as applied to learning systems
- Feedforward networks, regularization, and optimization algorithms
- Convolutional and recurrent architectures
- Practical methodology for building and debugging learning systems

## Takeaway

A book to return to rather than read front-to-back. The chapters on optimization and regularization remain the clearest treatment of why modern training works.

[Read online](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)
