# Physically Based Rendering

Matt Pharr, Wenzel Jakob, and Greg Humphreys' *Physically Based Rendering* (PBRT) is the definitive textbook on physically based rendering, structured around a complete, production-quality renderer.

## What it covers

- Geometry and transforms, shape sampling, and acceleration structures
- Radiometry, light transport, the rendering equation, and Monte Carlo integration
- Materials, textures, cameras, film, and light sources

## Takeaway

Reading the actual renderer source alongside the theory is the difference between understanding and really understanding a pipeline. The transport chapter is essential.
