# The Feynman Lectures on Physics

Feynman's lectures remain the gold standard for physical intuition — a masterclass in how to *think* about mechanics, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics rather than merely compute.

## What it covers

- Volume I: Mechanics, radiation, and heat
- Volume II: Electromagnetism and matter
- Volume III: Quantum mechanics

## Takeaway

The value is not the equations but the intuition behind them. Reading even a few chapters permanently changes how you reason about physics.
