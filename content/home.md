## Research Interests

- **Object-centric representation** — slot attention, compositional state representations, and relation modelling for reasoning and planning
- **Efficient multimodal learning** — multi-teacher knowledge distillation, tokenizer-free fine-tuning, hierarchical attention, latent-state compression, and KV-cache reduction
- **Self-supervised world models** — JEPA, DINO, CLIP, Barlow Twins and Siamese networks, alongside foveated processing and saliency-driven vision
- **Embodied &amp; agentic systems** — visual-text grounding for computer-use agents, incremental frame processing, vision-language-action models, and state-transition computation
- **Engineering foundations** — mechanical design, vibration analysis, control systems, and robot kinematics from configuration spaces to screw theory

## Current Focus

> Pushing object-centric state representations toward practical efficiency — a 22% inference improvement on CLEVR and its variants — while exploring state-transition computation that stores encoded updates in the KV cache instead of redundant context.
